# Virality playbook (the ig-reel / ig-caption / ig-human / ig-viral methods, adapted for Hindi word reels)

A reel is decided in the first 2 seconds and kept by the next 5. Instagram pushes a reel further when people watch it
to the end (and replay it), share it in DMs, save it, and follow from it. Everything below serves one of those.

## Hook tournament (N1)
Write THREE different hooks, each from a different formula, then use the strongest as N1. A hook:
- is said in under 2.5 seconds: 4 to 9 words, the payload in the first 3 words, never a preamble
  (no "दोस्तों", "नमस्ते", "आज हम", "इस वीडियो में", "क्या आप जानते हैं", "एक बात बताऊँ");
- has something concrete (a number, a time, an everyday object, the reel's own word) and something at stake
  (time, money, sleep, marks, a relationship, self-respect);
- talks to one viewer as "तुम" (a friend, not a crowd).
Formulas that suit this channel:
- **Negative command**: stop doing something everyone does. "रात को फ़ोन लेकर सोना बंद करो।"
- **Hidden in the word**: the lesson is inside the word itself. "'चिंता' शब्द में ही इसका इलाज छुपा है।"
- **One-letter claim**: "सिर्फ़ एक मात्रा, और पूरी ज़िंदगी का मतलब बदल गया।"
- **Sting question**: a question that hurts a little and has a "you". "तुम्हारा 'कल' कितने साल से कल ही है?"
- **Number + cost**: "हर दिन 3 घंटे… सिर्फ़ एक आदत की वजह से।"
- **Nobody tells you**: "स्कूल में ये कोई नहीं बताता…"
- **Identity call**: names exactly who it is for. "जो हर बार 'बस पाँच मिनट' बोलते हैं, ये तुम्हारे लिए है।"
- **Before/after**: "पहले मैं हर काम टालता था। अब…"

## Watch-through and replay
- Change something on screen every 2 to 3 seconds (a write, the red part, the person moves): no line over ~4 s.
- The word change (the "aha") lands between 40% and 60% of the reel, never in the last seconds.
- Last line echoes the hook's words so the loop back to N1 feels seamless (a replay counts as a full watch).
- 25 to 40 s total. Shorter is fine if the idea is complete.

## Shares and saves (what grows a new channel)
- One ask only, and make it a SHARE to a named person ("जिसे आज ये सुनना था…", "उस दोस्त को भेजो जो…") or a one-word comment.
- The lesson must be sendable: something a viewer would forward to a friend, sibling or parent without explaining.

## Caption (job A: the reel already hooked them)
- Line 1 (shows before "more", at most 125 characters): the ask or a question that invites a ONE-word comment.
  Never starts with a hashtag or an emoji; not a copy of the hook.
- Then 2 to 3 short paragraphs. Use both words of the change in plain sentences (Instagram search reads captions),
  plus natural search words people type: शब्द, मतलब, हिंदी, सीख, ज़िंदगी, मोटिवेशन.
- Exactly 3 hashtags on the last line, specific topic labels (never #viral #fyp #explore #trending).
- No links, at most 2 emoji, no em dash (—), no "AI voice": no "आइए जानते हैं", "यकीन मानिए", "जादुई", "सफ़र" filler.

## Sound human (ig-human)
Write how a 22-year-old elder sibling talks, not a textbook: short lines, everyday words (English words Indians
actually say are fine: फ़ोन, टाइम, एग्ज़ाम), contractions of speech, no ornate Sanskrit, no em dashes, no lists of three
adjectives, no "सच में अद्भुत" style praise.
