# Hook formulas working in the niche now

- **Caveat:** these come from caption starts only (no video seen), and 7 of the 9 outliers are from one 70k-follower account, so treat them as hints.
- **Word-play riddle question** (closest to our format; 3.25× its account's norm). Shape: `"___ चाहिए तो ___ कैसे?"`, where the two words sound alike (like आसमान / आसान). Why it travels: the sound-alike pair makes you stop and think, and the answer is a life lesson.
- **Command to learn a virtue** (10.19×, the biggest). Shape: `"___ रखना सीखो"`. Why it travels: it's short and clear, and the viewer sends it to someone who needs it. It's the same theme as our सब्र reel, which flopped, so the difference is likely in the delivery. Test it with a tighter hook.
- **Nostalgia question** (7.16×). Shape: `"___ को कितना याद करते हो?"` Why it travels: it asks for a feeling everyone shares, so people comment and tag.
- **Rhyming defiance line** (2.64×). Shape: `"___ पे अड़े, वो ___ रहे"`, where the two key words share a sound. Why it travels: it's easy to remember, and it sounds like a saying.
- **Time-box challenge** (2.4×). Shape: `"आज से ___ घंटा सिर्फ़ ___"`. Why it travels: it gives a small, doable action the viewer can start today.
- **Gentle accusation with a metaphor** (3.76×). Shape: `"थोड़ा-थोड़ा आप ___ तोड़ रहे हो"`. Why it travels: it makes the viewer feel seen without a harsh scolding, and invites a share to "that person".
- **Dedication series** (2.3× on a small 1147-follower account). Shape: `"दिन __/100: यह ___ उन सबके नाम जो ___"`. Why it travels: a counter gives people a reason to follow, and the dedication gives them someone to send it to.
- **Festival greeting** (5.18×). Shape: `"Happy ___ 🪔"`. Why it travels: timing, not craft, and it works only on the day. Use it only if a festival lines up with a word pair (e.g. दीप / दीपक).
