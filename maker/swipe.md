# Hook formulas working in the niche now

- **Caveat:** only caption starts were collected, so these formulas are inferred from captions, not from the video hooks. Most outliers come from one 69,620-follower account, so they reflect one creator's style.
- **Consequence warning** (49.4x): `{आदत} करते रहोगे, तो {क़ीमती चीज़} दूर हो जाएगी।` It names a habit and a loss in one line, so the viewer thinks of a person who does this and shares it. It fits the channel: pair it with a word where one letter shows the loss.
- **Rhyme or sound twist** (4.9x and 3.4x): `{छोटी चीज़} नहीं, {बड़ी चीज़} पकड़ो` with matching sounds, or `{A} पूरे करो, {B} से पहले करो`. It is wordplay, close to this channel's own craft, and it is easy to remember and quote.
- **Not-A-but-B reframe** (7.0x): `{चीज़} {A} से नहीं, {B} से आती है।` It is one clean, saveable idea and a good fit for a letter-swap reveal.
- **Own-your-choice permission** (9.3x): `{अपने फ़ैसलों} के साथ खुश रहो, {दुनिया की राय} छोड़ो।` It gives the viewer relief and permission, and works well as a send-to-a-friend line.
- **Bare curiosity teaser** (137.5x): `कुछ {अलग/नया} ...` with almost no caption. The caption gives nothing away, so the voiceover or visual probably did the work. We can't tell why it travelled. Treat it as a weak hint and test it only once.
- **Plain-man identity** (2.4x): `मैं {साधारण} हूँ, पर {साधारण} बनकर नहीं रहना।` It lets viewers state who they are and want to be, which people share.
- **Day counter with dedication** (2.3x, 1,141-follower account): `{n}/100 — ये उनके नाम जो {अकेला महसूस करते हैं}।` It is a series format plus a named audience. The lift is modest, but it is a small account, so it shows the format can work without a big following.
