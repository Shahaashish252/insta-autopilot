# Hook formulas working in the niche now

- **Caveat:** both outliers come from one small account (1,152 followers), at 2.43x and 2.34x its own average. This is a hint about one account, not a niche trend, and neither reel is word-play.
- **Formula: "Dedication to Maa"** (most promising). Shape: `ये रील [माँ / किसी ख़ास रिश्ते] के नाम — [एक भावना] जो [रिश्ता] ही समझता है।` Why it travels: it gives the viewer a specific person to tag or send it to, and the feeling is universal. Word-play version to try: a pair that ends on माँ, e.g. माँ → माँग/माना, with a line about what she asked for.
- **Formula: "Day X of the challenge"** Shape: `[N]/100 ✅ — आज [N]वाँ दिन, [एक बात जो आज सीखी]।` Why it travels: the counter signals a series, which gives people a reason to follow and come back, and the repeated format makes each reel feel part of something bigger.
- **Formula: "For the friends who feel alone"** (audience callout). Shape: `ये उन दोस्तों के नाम जिन्हें लगता है कि [दर्द/अकेलापन]।` Why it travels: viewers who see themselves in the first line stay, and many send it on to someone who feels the same.
- **Formula: "Promise line as a hook"** Shape: `एक दिन मैं [किसी अपने] के क़दमों में [सबसे बड़ी चीज़] रख दूँगा…` Why it travels: a vow is emotional and quotable, and it works as an opening line or card.
- **Formula: "Place as proof"** Shape: `आज [सार्वजनिक जगह] से शूट की गई…` Why it travels: it adds a bit of realness and effort. Treat it as the weakest hint, since it is only a detail of that account's style.
