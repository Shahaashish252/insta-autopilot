# What works on this channel

- **Very little data:** there is only 1 reel (155 views, 37.5 s). Everything below is a hint to test, not a finding. Nothing can be compared yet, because the 1.0 against the median is just the reel against itself.
- **Tracking gaps:** reach, saves, total interactions and watch time all show 0, and the hook fields are empty. These are most likely not being captured rather than truly zero. Fix the tracking first, because without watch time and saves we cannot say whether the hook holds people.
- **MORE (hint): the share ask in the first line of the caption.** The caption opens with a send-to-a-friend ask ("ये रील उस दोस्त को भेजो, जिसका सपना…"). The reel got 41 shares on 155 views, which is 264.5 per 1000 views. That is high in absolute terms, but we have nothing on this channel to compare it with.
- **MORE (hint): word pairs that are about a person the viewer knows.** सपना → अपना works as "send this to someone". Try more pairs with a natural recipient, e.g. वक़्त → वक्ता, रिश्ता → रिस्ता, हार → हारा/हर. Check each pair makes sense before using it.
- **MORE (hint): comments are strong too (71 per 1000 views).** Keep the reveal simple enough that people want to reply with their own word pair.
- **Length:** keep to about 35-38 s for now, as in the one reel we have. Do not draw conclusions about length from one reel.
- **Hook:** the hook line, card and formula were not recorded. From now on, write down the first spoken line and the on-screen card for every reel, so hook types can be compared after about 8-10 reels.
- **Saves:** 0 saves is probably missing data. Even so, nothing in the reel gives people a reason to save it. Add a closing line worth keeping, such as the one-line lesson written as a card.
- **STOP:** posting without logging the hook and formula, and treating a single reel's numbers as a pattern.
- **STOP:** copying the niche's shayari or Maa-themed content. It is a different format, and the outliers come from one tiny account.
- **Experiment for next week (one variable only):** post two reels with similar word pairs and the same length. Change only the first line of the caption, one with the friend-share ask and one with a question the viewer can answer in the comments, e.g. "तुम्हारा कौन सा शब्द बदलना है?". Compare shares and comments per 1000 views.
- **Judging the result:** with only a few reels, treat anything less than about 2x difference as noise.
