# What works on this channel

- **Only 3 reels, so everything below is a hint, not a rule.** Reach, saves and watch time are all 0 in the data. That looks like missing tracking, not proof that nobody watched or saved. I can't judge retention or saves at all.
- **Do more of the matra-add hook.** मन → मान used "एक मात्रा जोड़ो, ___ बन जाओ" and got 758 views, 2.65× the channel median of 286. It was the only reel with a hook score (79). One reel is a hint, not proof.
- **Hook card:** keep it short and promise a result, like "एक मात्रा से मान बनो". It's the same promise as the spoken hook, so the viewer gets it in under 2 seconds.
- **Word theme:** pick words where the new word is something a person wants to become or have (मान, अपना). Both reels had a positive ending. We haven't tried a warning type (चिंता → चिता), so I can't compare.
- **Keep the friend-tag ask.** Both older reels open the caption with "ये रील उस दोस्त को भेजो, जो ___" and filling the blank with a specific pain. The सपना → अपना reel got 41 shares and 12 comments on 163 views (about 251 shares per 1000 views). मन → मान got 3 shares on 758 views (about 4 per 1000). The सपना reel is a very small sample, and 3 likes next to 41 shares is odd, so check that number before trusting it.
- **Sharing and views are not the same thing here.** सपना → अपना was the most shared but had the fewest views (0.57× median). मन → मान was the opposite. We don't yet know which one the algorithm rewards more, so aim for both.
- **Hand-made vs not:** मन → मान was made by hand and सपना → अपना was not, but the sample is too small to say that matters. Don't change process because of it.
- **Length:** both scored reels are about 37.5 s. There is no short vs long contrast, so I can't recommend a change. Staying in the 25–40 s range is fine.
- **Too early to judge the रूप reel.** It was posted today with 286 views and 0 likes, comments and shares. Check again in 2–3 days before calling it a miss. Its caption ("जो खुद को सिर्फ़ शक्ल से नापते हैं…") describes the viewer in a negative way, so that is worth a look if it stays flat.
- **Stop:** posting reels with no hook line or hook card recorded. Two of three have missing hook data, so we can't learn from them. Always log the hook line, hook card and formula.
- **Also stop:** ending the caption start with an unfinished fragment ("…में बस एक अक्षर बद") as the only visible text. Put the payoff word early in the caption.
