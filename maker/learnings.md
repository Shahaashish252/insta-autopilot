# What works on this channel

- **Read this first:** there are only 9 reels, all posted 5-11 Nov, and views run from 52 to 1,513. Everything below is a hint, not a proof. Newer reels have also had less time to collect views.
- **Tracking gap:** reach, saves and watch time show 0 on every reel, which looks like missing data rather than real zeros. Check the insights connection before judging saves or retention. Until then, views, shares and comments are the only signals we have.
- **Do MORE of the one-letter claim hook (hint).** Our best reel, रूप → रूह (1,513 views, 32 shares, 21.2 shares per 1,000 views), opened with "सिर्फ़ एक अक्षर बदलो, रूप बन जाए रूह।" The matra-add fix, मन → मान (860 views), came next. Both are single reels, not a pattern yet.
- **Do MORE of identity and self-worth words (hint).** रूप → रूह, मन → मान and हार → हर all speak to how someone sees themselves, and they were our 3 best on views. धन → धुन is about money, and it did worst.
- **Hook score does not predict views here.** धन → धुन scored 100 and got 52 views, the lowest. रूप → रूह and मन → मान scored 79 and got the most views among the reels that have a score. Only 4 reels have scores, so don't write to the score.
- **STOP "stop doing X" openers (hint).** "सिर्फ़ पैसे के पीछे भागना बंद करो" (52 views) and the excuse call-out "तुम हर मौके पर नया बहाना बनाते हो" (599 views) scold the viewer. The best hooks promise a transformation instead: "X बन जाए Y".
- **Keep the "send this to someone" caption start, but know its limit.** Our top reel opened with "जो खुद को सिर्फ़ शक्ल से नापते हैं, ये रील उन्हें भेजो।" Naming a specific kind of person seems to help. Some small-view reels also had very high share rates: सपना → अपना got 41 shares on 167 views, कल → काल got 37 on 269 and चिंता → चिता got 27 on 225. So the ask earns shares, but shares have not turned into reach yet. Those rates sit on tiny view counts, so don't over-read them.
- **Comment prompts are unproven.** चिंता → चिता ("एक शब्द में कमेंट करो") got 2 comments, and कल → काल ("कमेंट में लिखो") got 0. सपना → अपना got 12 comments, but it is also the only reel not made by hand, so don't credit the prompt alone.
- **Length:** the 3 best reels ran 30-39 s. The longest, कल → काल at 44 s, got 269 views. Aim for roughly 30-39 s. This is a weak hint, since we have no watch-time data.
- **Name the new word in the first line.** Hooks that never show or say the change in the opening seconds (we have no hook data for हार, कल, चिंता, सब्र and सपना) can't be judged. Log the hook line and card for every reel so next week's review has something to compare.
- **Don't judge posting time or day.** Posts went out at many different hours, and each reel had a different amount of time to collect views.
- **One experiment for next week (one variable only):** post 3 reels with the same hook formula (one-letter claim), the same 35-39 s length and a self-worth word. Change only the caption's first line, alternating between a send ask ("ये रील उसे भेजो जो…") and a save ask ("इसे सेव कर लो…"). Compare shares and saves once the saves tracking works.
